# Site, biome, story and settlements

For detailed macro-to-micro landform sculpting, cliff geology, erosion, river banks, authored terrain gradients and vegetation, read [terrain-sculpting-masterclass.md](terrain-sculpting-masterclass.md) and [vegetation-and-custom-trees.md](vegetation-and-custom-trees.md).

A professional builder makes terrain and building read as **one composition**. Architectural style is not only the roof: it is where people approach, how water drains, how materials arrive and what changes over time.

## Site reconnaissance

Observe terrain at the footprint, around it and from the approaches. Document:
- relative elevations, steep faces, cut/fill implications and natural rock outcrops;
- water levels, flood risk, coast/rivers, banks and bridge connections;
- vegetation mass and tree silhouettes;
- dominant viewing direction, path network, roads and nearby landmarks;
- sunlight/shade and expected weather/culture;
- protected features, existing player builds and world boundaries.

Pick the site if not fixed; if fixed, design around its constraints rather than flattening away all context.

## Ground contact

A building should meet the land credibly:
- a level base on flat land may use a shallow plinth and steps;
- sloped land may use terraced footings, retaining walls, piers or a split-level plan;
- wet marshes favor elevated walkways and sheltered storage;
- riverside structures may align loading bays and mooring access with the actual bank.

Avoid floating corners, 90-degree freshly carved cube cliffs, abrupt dirt-to-stone outlines, isolated single-block columns and foliage that visibly clips the walls.

If terrain modification is requested, keep changed volume proportionate to the building. Prefer a few intentional terraces over removing an entire hillside.

## Building as a visible history

Define **who built it, for whom, using what, and what happened afterwards**. A blacksmith's forge implies ore/fuel delivery, smoke, heavy working surface, ventilation and a cart route. A watchtower implies sightline, protected entry, guard circulation and signals. A shrine implies procession and a sightline or symbolic center.

Express this story with architecture. Don't rely on written lore text to rescue a building with no physical signs of purpose.

### Intentional aging

Distinguish:
- construction period: core structure and main material;
- later extension: altered roof junction, shifted door, secondary cladding;
- repairs: localized fresh stone/timber;
- occupation: worn path, stacked fuel, water barrel, drainage;
- abandonment: structural collapse and natural reclamation.

Age is not a uniform random noise layer.

## Landscape passes

1. **Primary terrain**: foundation relation and grade.
2. **Movement network**: public entrance, service entrance, paths, bridge and stairs.
3. **Hardscape**: retaining walls, yards, quay or courtyard.
4. **Utilities**: well, fuel, loading, livestock, drainage, bins, workshops.
5. **Vegetation**: clusters guided by light, water and cultivation, not evenly scattered.
6. **Small narrative details**: believable objects near use zones, never every square meter.

Design exterior paths to lead somewhere. A street that ends in the middle of a facade is a failed connection.

## Settlement composition

Do not build a collection of identical homes with differently colored roofs. Establish a local architectural grammar (construction technique, shared foundation material, opening proportions), then vary by:
- occupation and status;
- plot size and terrain;
- build age and repair;
- public vs private exposure;
- proximity to gate, market, shoreline or fields.

Village hierarchy might include one communal landmark, a market building, working buildings and quieter dwellings. Preserve negative space for squares, sightlines and foot traffic.

### Composition from outside the town

Check whether multiple roofs merge into a single flat horizon or whether one landmark dominates by intention. Bridges, winding streets and roof gaps can frame the landmark.

## Biome is not a cosmetic filter

A taiga house is not automatically a plains house with spruce swapped in. Climate and resources can change its roof pitch, snow-sheltered entry, smoke treatment, wall thickness and storage plan. A desert settlement may rely on courtyards, sun shading and thick walls rather than conifer roof decoration.

For fantasy and magic, decide where technology departs from physics and make that departure **consistent and clearly authored**. A floating temple requires coherent magical support cues, not an accidental levitating foundation.

## Final site audit

Walk the intended route from far approach to entrance and interior. Observe from three secondary exterior angles. Does the building appear grown from its circumstances? Do landscaping, paths and utilities demonstrate use? If the answer depends on an explanatory paragraph that cannot be seen, revise the geometry.
