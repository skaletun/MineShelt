# Changelog

## 1.2.0 — 2026-10-20

### Added

- Dedicated handbook on **three-dimensional architectural relief of buildings themselves**, independently of landscape relief.
- Major mass → structural wall-section depth → microdetail → optical shadow hierarchy.
- Real recessed windows and doors, forward structural frames, useful bay windows, porches, arcade/arch volumes, column/buttress support, cornices, multi-volume roofs, eaves and finished undersides.
- Explicit depth/projection budgets, wall-section and elevation plans, bounding boxes, rotation/state/collision concerns, and reviews at rear/side/diagonal/low-angle viewpoints.
- Worked exercise restoring a visually flat river workshop through an actual riverside gallery instead of a facade texture overlay.
- Eight depth-specific acceptance scenarios (38 total); facade section/diagonal/underside evidence in review sheet; new reusable agent commission prompt.
- References to official Mojang advice and credited community layered-wall case studies.

### Changed

- Main Agent Skill mandates real volumetric design and multi-angle visual critique.
- Form, roofs/facades, interior, critique, build artifacts and visual research references integrated with architectural depth curriculum.
- Static validator now requires the architectural depth chapter and at least 38 scenarios.

### Note

This update is instructional. It does not claim completed Minecraft in-game builds or observed visual quality without actual world/client tools.


## 1.1.0 — 2026-10-20

### Added

- Professional block palette studio: swatches, lighting, directional color/texture gradients, material roles, ten candidate families and anti-“block vomit” rules.
- Professional terraforming: macro/meso/micro landform authoring, cliff spines, strata, talus, rivers, shorelines, terrain palettes and protected editing practices.
- Hand-crafted vegetation: trunks, roots, branch hierarchy, crown silhouettes, ecological placement and viewing corridors.
- Curated, creator-attributed external image/video/reference bibliography and visual analysis worksheet; no unlicensed third-party art copied into the repository.
- Human builder practice loop, structured decisions, visual feedback and correction drills.
- Two original worked studies focused on harbor terrain integration and palette correction.
- 15 additional skill evaluation scenarios (30 total) and evidence-based review sheet.
- Commission prompts in docs/USE.md.

### Changed

- MineShelt's main SKILL.md now requires relevant palette, terraforming, custom vegetation, source study and iterative review phases rather than treating them as optional decoration.
- Existing design, site, material and critique chapters cross-link the new professional references.
- Documentation identifies what can be actually inspected or built versus merely proposed.

### Limitations

This release delivers agent instructions and evaluative material, not an in-game connection, procedural generator or proof of aesthetic performance. Real-world/game build evaluations remain environment-dependent.
